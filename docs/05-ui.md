# 05. UI and motion

The look: Mistral's cream paper and pixel sunset, turned into a 16-bit fighting game set in San Francisco. A working reference of the arena and the Tower is in [prototype/arena.html](../prototype/arena.html). Open it in a browser.

## Borrowing the Mistral look

These tokens come from mistral.ai's own CSS (pulled November 2026). We borrow the palette, the square corners, the stacked-stripe motif and the pixel art. We don't use their logo, wordmark, mascots or their proprietary ALT Mistral font. The footer says "Built at the Mistral AI hackathon" in plain text.

What makes their site recognizable:

- Warm cream paper backgrounds, not white.
- A sunset ramp from yellow to deep red, often as stacked horizontal stripes with hard edges and no blur.
- Pixel-art landscapes.
- Square corners almost everywhere.
- Navy-black for dark surfaces, not pure black.
- Space Mono for small uppercase labels, Inter for body.

### Type

| Use | Font | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Fight text: ROUND 1, K.O., damage numbers, HUD names | Pixelify Sans (Google Fonts) | Always with a 2px ink outline in the arena |
| Labels, stats, seeds, build hash, odds | Space Mono, uppercase, letter-spacing 0.06em | Same mono Mistral uses |
| Headings and body | Inter, 600 for headings with -0.02em tracking | |

### Shapes and texture

- Border radius 0. Borders 2px ink. Hard offset shadows (`4px 4px 0 var(--ink)`), never blurred.
- The stripe motif: five stacked bands of `--sun-1` to `--sun-5`, hard edges. Use it for the hero, health bar fill, the round title cards, confetti and the champion's belt.
- Dithering (checkerboard of two colors) to blend between bands in pixel art, instead of gradients.

## The Tower

The bracket is vertical. Floor 1 at the bottom holds the opening bouts. The final sits at the top under the champion's belt. Fighters climb.

```
                 [  THE GOLDEN GATE BELT  ]
                            |
 FLOOR 3  FINAL        [ A  vs  F ]
                     ______|______
 FLOOR 2  SEMIS    [ A vs D ]  [ F vs G ]
                   ___|___      ___|___
 FLOOR 1  OPENERS [A v H][D v E][F v C][G v B]
```

- **Rows** are rounds. A left rail labels each floor in Space Mono ("FLOOR 1 / OPENERS / 4 BOUTS").
- **Match cards** show two fighter rows (24px sprite, name, seed, tier) and a status pill: `BETTING OPEN` with a countdown, `LOCKED`, `LIVE`, `FINAL`, `BYE`, `WALKOVER`.
- **Connectors** are stepped pixel lines, drawn as SVG paths with only horizontal and vertical segments. The winner's path lights up in the sun ramp. The loser's row greys out with a crack sprite.
- **Live match** pulses and gets a `LIVE` flag. The page auto-scrolls to it unless the user scrolled away in the last 10 seconds.
- **Climb animation**: when a match completes, the winner's sprite hops up the connector to its next slot (about 1.2s, three hops), with stripe confetti.
- **Sealed matches look exactly like computing ones.** No hint of the result until it airs.
- **Sizes**: 4 to 64 entrants. Past 16, floors 1 and 2 collapse into a horizontally scrollable strip with a minimap.
- **Mobile**: each floor becomes a horizontal scroll-snap row. The Tower stays vertical. Tap a card to open the match.
- **Keyboard**: arrow keys move between match cards, Enter opens.

## Fighter card

Trading-card proportions (5:7).

- Header band in the corner color with seed and tier badge (S, A, B, C).
- Sprite standing on a small stripe-sunset backdrop.
- Idea title and one-liner.
- Archetype (Juggernaut, Fortress, Gunslinger) with its signature weapon icon.
- Per-round letter grades before elimination (MARKET A, MOAT B, EXECUTION C). After elimination or the final, the full nine stats appear as pixel bars with the weigh-in rationale.
- Footer: `BUILD a1b2c3 · SAME AGENT AS EVERY FIGHTER`.
- VC hot takes (v2) as a sticky-note strip.

### HUD

- **Health bars**: 20 segments of 5 HP. Fill uses the stripe ramp: `--sun-1` on top, `--sun-4` on the bottom edge. Below 30% the bar flashes between `--sun-5` and `--sun-6` once per second.
- **Ghost drain**: on a hit the lost chunk turns `--paper` and stays for 400ms, then drains over 300ms. Standard fighting-game trick. It makes damage size readable.
- **Round indicator**: `R1 MARKET` with three criterion chips. The chip for the shot in flight lights up.
- **Build hash** under the round indicator, always visible.
- **Captions** at the bottom: shooter name in corner color, the shot's headline (12 words max), evidence chips (`E4` with a green tick if supported, grey if estimate, red strike if fabricated), and the damage dealt.
- **Receipts** button opens a right drawer listing every shot so far with the full claim, judge verdict, judge note and sources. During a live broadcast the drawer only shows shots that have already aired.

### Arena scene

- Render at 480x270 internal resolution and scale up with nearest-neighbor to fit. It looks properly pixel, and it's cheap.
- Sky in stacked sun-ramp bands. Sun low on the horizon. Golden Gate towers, Sutro Tower and the Transamerica Pyramid in navy silhouette. Bay water reflects the bands in broken stripes.
- A fog bank sits off to the side. It rolls in during sudden death.
- Fighters stand on two piers or rooftops, left and right.

### Fighters

Procedural pixel war machines, generated from `sprite_seed`. A fighter is assembled from parts:

| Part | Options |
|---|---|
| Base | Tracks, two legs, four legs, wheels, hover pad |
| Body | Tower, box, dome, wedge |
| Weapon mount | Arm, turret, shoulder pod |
| Banner | Idea initials on a flag in the corner color |
| Accent | Hue from the idea's category |

Frames: idle bob (2 frames), recoil (1), hit flash (1 frame white), staggered (below 50% HP, smoke puffs), critical (below 25%, sparks and small flames), K.O. (pixel dissolve).

The weapon fired depends on the criterion, not on the fighter. Both corners use the same arsenal.

## Arena choreography

The arena plays the broadcast script and nothing else. Every visual beat maps to a cue from the director.

### Weapons by criterion

| Round | Criterion | Weapon | Projectile | Travel |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| MARKET | problem | Flame lance | Stream of fire pixels, "HAIR ON FIRE" on a crit | 450ms |
| MARKET | market | Ballista | Big bolt on a high arc, heavy screen shake on impact | 1000ms |
| MARKET | wtp | Coin cannon | Burst of gold coins that bounce | 700ms |
| MOAT | differentiation | Laser rifle | Instant straight beam with afterglow | 150ms |
| MOAT | moat | Trebuchet | Boulder on a very high arc. Parry raises a stone wall. | 1100ms |
| MOAT | timing | Clockwork railgun | Three tick charge-up, then a fast slug with gear particles | 300ms + 450ms charge |
| EXECUTION | gtm | Gatling gun | 6 to 10 small rounds in a stream | 600ms |
| EXECUTION | feasibility | Rocket | Wobbly path, smoke trail | 900ms |
| EXECUTION | scale | Orbital beam | Column of light from the sky onto the target | 500ms + 300ms warning |

Projectile size scales with impact: impact 1 is 0.7x, impact 2 is 1x, impact 3 is 1.4x.

### Shot outcomes

| Verdict | Visual | Sound |
|---|---|---|
| `landed` | Impact flash, target hit-flashes, damage number floats up | Weapon hit |
| `landed`, impact 3, supported evidence | Critical. 80ms hit-stop, 6px shake, "CRITICAL" stamp | Heavy hit |
| `partial` | Smaller impact, number in `--sun-2` | Light hit |
| `parried` | Hex shield (or stone wall in the moat round) pops in front of the target. Projectile deflects up. "PARRIED". Caption switches to the defense headline. | Clang |
| `glancing` | Sparks off armor, "GLANCING", 0 | Ping |
| `whiff` | Projectile sails past, "MISS" | Whoosh |
| Fabricated | Weapon jams, two smoke puffs, explodes on the shooter. Red "FACT-CHECKED" stamp. Caption shows the bad claim struck through. | Fizzle, then boom |
| Judges' hammer | A pixel gavel drops from the top of the screen onto the weaker side. Caption: "Judges: Fogcast's market sizing was top-down only." | Gavel |

### Timing per shot (1x)

```
0      windup / recoil                 350ms
350    projectile travel               150 to 1100ms by weapon
+      impact                          250ms
+      damage number float             700ms, overlaps the next windup
       caption stays up                at least 2200ms per shot
```

Captions set the pace, not projectiles. A 12-word headline needs a little over two seconds to read, so each shot slot is at least 2.2s. That's why shots carry a short `headline` separate from the full claim.

A round of about 12 shots runs around 30 seconds. With intros, round cards and the finish, a match is about 2 minutes.

### Match sequence

1. **Walk-in** (loops while computing): fighters roll in from the edges, crowd murmur, the tale of the tape card (tiers, archetypes, one-liners side by side).
2. **Round title**: stripe band wipes across, "ROUND 1 / MARKET", the three criteria with weights. 1.5s.
3. **Shots** per the script.
4. **Round scorecard**: a card slides up with one row per criterion. Each row shows both scores, a margin bar, rubric points and HP dealt. A footnote reads "Judged twice, positions swapped. Agreement 92%." 4s.
5. Repeat for rounds 2 and 3.
6. **Outcome**:
   - `KO`: the final blow plays at 0.25x speed for 1.2s. The screen flashes twice (never more than 3 flashes per second). "K.O." stamps in. The loser dissolves into pixels that a wind gust carries off-screen, a nod to the mistral wind.
   - `DECISION`: both fighters stand. A judges' card flips: "DECISION, UNANIMOUS". The loser kneels.
   - `SUDDEN_DEATH`: fog rolls in off the bay. Both bars pulse red. One closing shot each, then the judges' vote tally (3 to 1). The final blow, then the K.O. sequence.
   - `PERFECT`: extra stamp if the winner took no damage.
7. **Winner pose** with stripe confetti and "WINNER ADVANCES". Button: "Why did they win?" opens the film room.
8. **Climb** plays on the Tower.

### Camera and feel

- Screen shake: amplitude in internal pixels (2 for normal hits, 6 for crits and ballistas), decays over 200ms.
- Hit-stop only on crits and the final blow.
- Slight zoom (1.05x) toward the target on crits.
- Idle crowd silhouettes along the bottom edge bob to the beat and jump on crits.

## Film room (trace viewer)

The answer to "why did this idea win?" Opens from the match screen after the match completes, or from any finished match on the Tower.

1. **Verdict header**: outcome, winner, rubric gap ("won by 13.3 rubric points"), what decided it ("Decided by: scores" or "Decided by: sudden death judge, 3 to 1"), and the three-bullet fight report.
2. **Scorecard**: one row per criterion. Columns: weight, A score (AB order, BA order, final), B score (same), margin, rubric points, HP. Stability flags inline. Clicking a row expands both judge rationales for both fighters, caps applied, and the evidence they leaned on.
3. **Timeline**: rounds, volleys, shots. Each shot shows its full claim, type, criterion, evidence chips with fact-check verdicts, judge verdict and note, and damage. Defenses link to the shot they answered.
4. **Evidence locker**: every source with URL, snippet, who found it (dossier or scouting) and which shots cited it.
5. **Raw calls**: every LLM call for the match with role, model, seed, tokens, latency, cost, and the request and response as collapsible JSON.
6. **Export**: "Copy as Markdown" so founders can take their report card home.

## Bet slip

Shown on the match screen and on Tower cards while betting is open.

- Two big buttons, one per fighter, in corner colors, each with current decimal odds ("2.40x").
- Stake chips: 10, 50, 100, 500, MAX. A custom amount field.
- Estimated payout with a note that odds move until the bell.
- A countdown to the bell. At zero the slip stamps "BETS LOCKED".
- After settlement: a coin shower on a win, a small "REKT" on a loss.
- Wallet balance in the header ("S$ 1,240").

## Host console and Arena Mode

- **Console**: tournament setup (size, preset, seeding, schedule, budget), entrant table with moderation and duplicate flags, brief approvals, weigh-in progress, per-match controls (open betting, lock, air, retry, void), eval-health numbers, spend.
- **Arena Mode**: a 16:9 full-screen view for the projector. The arena takes 70% of the width, a mini Tower on the right, a bet-volume ticker and a QR code to join along the bottom. Sound on by default. Keyboard: `B` opens betting on the next match, `L` locks, `Space` airs, `R` replays the last match, `T` toggles the Tower.

## Accessibility

- `prefers-reduced-motion`: no shake, no hit-stop, no flashes, no slow-mo. Projectiles become quick fades. The fight still reads through captions and bars.
- Flashes stay at or under 3 per second (WCAG 2.3.1).
- Captions are always on in the arena.
- Corners are distinguished by color, side and icon.
- An `aria-live="polite"` region announces hits: "Fogcast takes 18 damage on market size. Fogcast at 49."
- The Tower, the film room and the bet slip are fully keyboard-navigable.
- Contrast: text on `--paper` uses `--ink` or `--ink-3`. Never put body text on the sun ramp.

## Sound

- 8-bit SFX per weapon and per outcome, a crowd loop, a heartbeat for sudden death.
- Announcer (v1): Voxtral TTS lines pre-generated per match at seal time ("Round one. Market!", "PermitPilot with the ballista!", "K.O.!"). Pre-generating keeps playback on the shared clock.
- Muted by default on the web, unmuted in Arena Mode.

## Performance

- The canvas runs at a fixed 480x270 resolution. A particle pool caps at about 400 live particles. A low-power mode drops particles to 25% when the frame time stays above 20ms.
- Pause the render loop when the tab is hidden. On return, re-sync from the broadcast clock by applying missed cues instantly.
- Lazy-load PixiJS only on the match screen and in Arena Mode.
