# 05. UI and motion

The look: Mistral's cream paper and pixel sunset, turned into a 16-bit fighting game set in San Francisco. A working reference of the arena and the Tower is in [prototype/arena.html](../prototype/arena.html). Open it in a browser.

## Borrowing the Mistral look

These tokens come from mistral.ai's own CSS (pulled November 2026). We borrow the palette, the square corners, the stacked-stripe motif and the pixel art. We don't use their logo, wordmark, mascots or their proprietary ALT Mistral font. The footer says "Built at the Mistral AI hackathon" in plain text.

What makes their site recognizable:

- Warm cream paper backgrounds, not white.
- A sunset ramp from yellow to deep red, often as stacked horizontal stripes with hard edges and no blur.
- Pixel-art landscapes.
- Square corners almost everywhere.
- Navy-black for dark surfaces, not pure black.
- Space Mono for small uppercase labels, Inter for body.

### Type

| Use | Font | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Fight text: ROUND 1, K.O., damage numbers, HUD names | Pixelify Sans (Google Fonts) | Always with a 2px ink outline in the arena |
| Labels, stats, seeds, build hash, odds | Space Mono, uppercase, letter-spacing 0.06em | Same mono Mistral uses |
| Headings and body | Inter, 600 for headings with -0.02em tracking | |

### Shapes and texture

- Border radius 0. Borders 2px ink. Hard offset shadows (`4px 4px 0 var(--ink)`), never blurred.
- The stripe motif: five stacked bands of `--sun-1` to `--sun-5`, hard edges. Use it for the hero, health bar fill, the round title cards, confetti and the champion's belt.
- Dithering (checkerboard of two colors) to blend between bands in pixel art, instead of gradients.

## Design language additions

The battle revamp adds these to the tokens and shapes above.

- **Element colors.** Market is `--sun-2`. Moat is stone, `#8FA3B8` on night and `#56566C` on paper. Execution is `--sun-5`. Each also carries a 16x16 pixel icon, so color is never the only cue.
- **Night for the show, paper for reading.** The arena, the Tower and Arena Mode use the night theme. Fighter cards, receipts and the film room use paper.
- **Motion.** Screen changes use a five-band stripe wipe. UI motion uses `steps()` easing so it moves like pixel art. Buttons press into their hard shadow. Narration types at 40 characters per second and completes instantly under reduced motion.
- **Stage time of day.** Round 1 is sunset, round 2 is dusk with fog on the bay, round 3 is night with lit windows. Sudden death brings the fog in and one spotlight.
- **Parallax.** The skyline is three layers that shift a few pixels with the camera.
- **Type scale.** Pixelify Sans at 64, 40, 28 and 20. Space Mono labels at 11 to 13. Inter body at 15 and 17.
- **Icons.** A 16x16 pixel icon set drawn in code: coin, wall, rocket, shield, gavel, receipt, crown, briefcase, megaphone.
- **Sound.** Short synthesized 8-bit effects through Web Audio, no audio files. Menu tick, select, one sound per weapon, hit, crit, parry, K.O. and a promotion jingle. Muted by default with a toggle.

`prefers-reduced-motion` and the flash limit still apply to everything above. No animation may flash more than 3 times in a second.

## The Tower

The Tower is the org chart. Every startup starts as the head of its own company. Beat a company and you acquire it: the loser, with every company it already owns, moves under the winner, and the winner moves up one title. The last one standing is CEO with every other entrant beneath it. A viewer should be able to watch a company's org grow as it wins.

The screen uses the night theme.

### Rules on screen

- Roots sit on the top row in bracket order, left to right. Each root's reports hang below it as a tidy tree with connectors made only of horizontal and vertical segments. A parent is centred over its reports.
- A tile shows the mascot, the company name, the title badge with headcount and the clout score. Alive roots are bright and slightly larger. Acquired tiles are dimmer and carry a small `ACQUIRED BY {name}` line, or `WALKOVER` when the absent entrant was a walkover.
- Two adjacent roots that meet next are joined by a VS link. The link carries the match status pill: `BETTING OPEN`, `LOCKED`, `LIVE` or `FINAL`, with `UP NEXT` for a scheduled match. The link is a button that opens the match. A live link pulses.
- Sealed results never show. The screen draws only what the chart and the public match list say, so a match that is not `complete` looks exactly like an unplayed one. No winner appears anywhere until the result is public.
- When `championId` is set the CEO tile wears the belt and a crown, the page shows `CHAMPION`, and the full company chart sits below it.

### Acquisition animation

When a match completes, the loser's tile and its whole subtree slide under the winner while the connectors redraw. The winner's title badge flips to the next rung, its headcount counts up, an `ACQUIRED` stamp lands, stripe confetti falls, and the promotion jingle plays if sound is on. The whole beat takes about 2.5 seconds.

Under `prefers-reduced-motion` it becomes a short cross fade with the same end state. The screen also announces the acquisition in a polite live region: winner acquired loser, winner promoted to the next title, winner now runs a company of N.

### Floors rail

A left rail labels the title ladder for this tournament. An 8-entrant field runs CEO, President, VP, Director. Every rung is listed with the number of companies holding it. Rungs earned so far are lit, and the highest rung reached so far is marked. The rail narrows to fit on a phone.

### Bracket toggle

A toggle switches to the classic vertical bracket built from the match list. The final sits at the top under the belt, the openers at the bottom, connectors are stepped, and a winner's row lights up once the match is `complete`. Round labels read FINAL, SEMIS, OPENERS and so on, with the bout count.

### Sizes

The Tower works from 2 to 64 entrants. Tiles shrink at 9 entrants and again at 17. Past 8 roots the chart scrolls horizontally inside its stage, shows a `SCROLL` hint and snaps to tiles. At 390 px the floors rail narrows and the chart scrolls one tile at a time.

### Interaction

- Click or tap a tile to open the fighter card.
- Click or tap a VS link to open the match.
- Arrow keys move between tiles by geometry. Enter or Space opens the focused tile. Every tile and link is a real button whose name includes the company, title, headcount and, when relevant, who acquired it.

## Fighter card

Trading-card proportions (5:7), paper theme.

- Header band with the seed, the tier badge (S, A, B, C) and the org title badge with headcount.
- The mascot on a stripe-sunset backdrop, with its class and category ("Clerk / proptech"). The class is derived from the category, so the card names it.
- Company name and one-liner.
- Clout meter with tier, then the line "Clout is for show. It never affects a fight."
- "Runs a company of N" once the entrant has acquired anyone.
- Founders: one generated crew figure per founder, with name, role and handles. Never a photo.
- Launch posts from the entrant profile: source (HN, X, WEB), title or first line, the main metric and a link. Post text is untrusted, so it renders as plain text only. Only `http` and `https` links become anchors, and they open in a new tab with `rel="noopener noreferrer"`. A card shows two posts and counts any others.
- Footer: `BUILD {hash} · SAME AGENT AS EVERY FIGHTER`, under a stripe rule.

## Match screen

The match screen is one turn-based battle. Each shot in the broadcast script is a turn: one side is lit, picks a move from a sealed menu, makes its claim, and fires. The screen answers four questions at every moment: whose turn it is, what move was used, what was argued, and what it did to the bars.

### HUD

Two plates, one per corner, sit above the stage. The left plate is red (`--corner-a`), the right plate is blue (`--corner-b`).

- **Plate**: org title badge, seed and tier, name in the pixel face, and a 6 px edge in the corner colour so the sides separate at a glance, in greyscale too.
- **Health bar**: 20 segments of 5 HP. Filled segments use the corner colour; empty segments sit on `--paper-2` with ink borders. The number is set beside the bar.
- **Ghost drain**: the lost chunk is a paper coloured span under the segments. It holds for 260 ms, then drains over 300 ms. Damage size reads from the gap, not from arithmetic.
- **Low health**: below 30 HP the bar pulses once per second. The pulse is brightness, not colour alone.
- **Statuses**: `MOMENTUM`, `EXPOSED` and `LAST STAND` badges sit in a reserved row under the number, so the stage never shifts when one appears. They turn on and off only from `status` cues.
- **Round plate** (centre): `ROUND n NAME`, the round's three criterion chips with integer weights, the active criterion filled, and the line `SAME AGENT BOTH CORNERS` beside the director version. Both fighters run the same hashed build, so the line is a promise, not a label.

### Turn strip

One pip per turn, grouped by round and coloured by the corner that acts. Aired pips are solid in the actor's colour, the current pip is raised on `--sun-2` with an ink outline, and unaired pips keep their actor colour as a bottom edge. The pip number is always visible, so colour is never the only cue. Clicking a pip jumps to that turn, playing or paused. Groups are labelled `R1`, `R2`, `R3`, with the round name in the tooltip.

### Spotlight

On turn start the actor is lit by a soft cone and a warm pool at the feet, and the other side drops to 45% opacity behind a gradient wash. This is the main attention cue: if you look away and back, the lit side tells you whose turn it is. Nothing else on screen changes as much.

### Command deck

The deck is the bottom third of the screen, under the strip.

- **Left, the move menu**: the actor's `menu` from the shot cue, in the order the fighter wrote it, each row with its element icon and colour, the move name and its type (`pitch`, `attack`, `defense`). A caret steps to the chosen move during the menu phase, then the row presses in and takes a tick and a select sound. The menu is a replay, and the line `SEALED MATCH. MOVES WERE WRITTEN BEFORE THE BELL.` sits under it so nobody reads it as a live choice.
- **Right, the narration**: a `NarrationBox` types `{Name} used {MOVE}!` at 40 characters per second, then the verdict line. The tag reads `Turn 03` during the call-out and `Verdict` after impact.
- **Facts row**: criterion, volley and damage. Damage stays sealed as `DAMAGE SEALED` until the impact phase.

### Speech bubble

The shot's `headline` appears in a speech bubble over the actor, with one chip per evidence id (`E1 SUPPORTED`, `E3 PARTIAL`, `E404 FABRICATED`) and a meta line for the move and criterion. Supported is green, partial is sun, estimate is grey, fabricated is red with a strike. On a parry the bubble stays with the attacker and a second panel on the defender's side shows the defense move and its headline, so the two claims face each other.

### Statuses

Statuses are cosmetic. They never change damage, HP or the rubric.

| Status | Appears | Clears | Tint |
|---|---|---|---|
| `momentum` | Two of the fighter's own turns land damage in a row with no damage taken between | On taking damage, or at the end of the round | Corner colour rim on the base plate |
| `exposed` | The fighter's claim was fact-checked | End of the match | Red hatch on the plate edge |
| `last_stand` | Display HP at 25 or below and still standing | End of the match | Sun stripe under the feet |

The plate badge and the fighter tint always land together, so the state reads in the corner and in the arena.

### Receipts

The receipts panel sits beside the stage on wide screens and below it under 1080 px. One row per aired turn, newest first: turn number, actor in the corner colour, damage, move with its element chip, outcome tag, headline, evidence chips and the verdict line. Rows for turns that have not aired are never rendered, which is what keeps results sealed: the DOM does not contain them. The newest row pulses once. The list scrolls, and fades at the bottom edge.

### Controls

Play and pause, speed 1x, 2x and 4x, next turn, restart and a sound toggle, plus a clock and a scrub bar marked with every turn start. Space toggles play, Right arrow goes to the next turn, Left arrow steps back two seconds. The screen pauses itself when the tab is hidden.

### Arena scene

- Render at 480x270 internal resolution and scale up with nearest-neighbor to fit. It looks properly pixel, and it's cheap.
- Sky in stacked sun-ramp bands. Sun low on the horizon. Golden Gate towers, Sutro Tower and the Transamerica Pyramid in navy silhouette. Bay water reflects the bands in broken stripes.
- Time of day follows the round: sunset for round 1, dusk with a light fog bank for round 2, night with stars and lit windows for round 3, night with heavy fog and one spotlight for sudden death. The spotlight is a stage light on the actor, separate from the scene.
- Two piers, one under each fighter, with the crowd silhouettes behind them. Parallax runs in three layers when the camera moves.

### Fighters

Each startup fights as a procedural pixel mascot, built from the sprite seed and the idea's category. No image files. The native grid is 48x48, drawn at 3x on the 480x270 stage, so a mascot stands about 144 px tall on a 270 px screen. Two fighters at that size fill the frame and read as a face-off.

The category maps to one of eight classes. Unknown categories hash to a stable class.

| Class | Categories | Silhouette |
|---|---|---|
| Clerk | proptech, civic, govtech, legal | Tall figure with a visor and an oversized rubber stamp |
| Gale | climate, energy, weather | Bird-like body with turbine wings |
| Vault | fintech, payments, insurance | Squat safe on legs with a dial for an eye |
| Daemon | devtools, infra, ai, security | Small imp with a terminal for a head |
| Courier | marketplace, logistics, commerce | Lean runner with a crate backpack |
| Medic | health, bio, wellness | Rounded body with a cross antenna and scanner arm |
| Rig | hardware, robotics, mobility | Wide tracked machine with a crane arm |
| Chatter | consumer, social, media, education | Speech-bubble head on a small body with antennae |

Rules for the art:

- A class must read from its silhouette alone. Fill the sprite with one flat color and all eight still separate.
- The seed varies proportions, one of three body variants, an accent hue, eyes and one accessory. Two fighters of the same class still look different.
- Corner color goes on trim and the base plate. The body keeps its class material, so identity survives a corner swap.
- States: idle (4 frames), windup, lunge, hurt (recoil plus one white frame), guard, staggered below 50 HP, critical below 25 HP, K.O. (dissolve into pixels carried off by the wind) and victory hop.
- The weapon fired depends on the criterion, not on the fighter. Both corners use the same arsenal.

### Crew

The people behind the idea stand behind their mascot as a crew of 1 to 4 figures, 12x16 pixels, generated from each founder's handle. They are generated figures, never photos. They idle, cheer a hit, flinch a backfire and walk. Members acquired in earlier matches stand further back than the fighter's own founders, so the crew reads as an org chart with history. When a fighter wins, the loser's crew crosses the stage in front of the mascots and joins the winner's side under an `ACQUIRED` stamp, so the acquisition is visible in the arena.

## Arena choreography

The arena plays the broadcast script and nothing else. Every visual beat maps to a cue from the director.

### Turn sequence

One shot cue is one turn. The cue's slot runs from its `t` to the next non-status cue's `t`, which is 3600 ms at 1x, so the fight keeps a steady beat.

| Phase | 1x | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| Turn start | 300 ms | Spotlight moves, the other side dims, the strip advances, `TURN n` and the actor's name stamp in |
| Menu | 600 ms | The deck shows the actor's sealed menu, the cursor steps to the chosen move, presses it, tick and select sound |
| Call-out | 900 ms | Narration types `{Name} used {MOVE}!`, the speech bubble drops with the claim and evidence chips, the camera pushes in to 1.06x |
| Attack | 480 to 1100 ms | Windup and lunge, the weapon fires, the camera follows the shot to the target |
| Impact | 400 ms | Hit-stop on crits, shake, damage number, ghost drain, receipt row slides in, stamp for the outcome |
| Verdict | 900 ms | Narration types the verdict line, the damage number stays up, statuses update when a `status` cue fires |

A weapon that needs more than 480 ms keeps its travel time and the five other phases share the rest of the slot, so a trebuchet or an orbital shot never overruns its cue. At 2x and 4x every phase scales with playback speed and the sequence of states does not change.

### Weapons by criterion

| Round | Criterion | Weapon | Projectile | Travel |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| MARKET | problem | Flame lance | Stream of fire pixels, "HAIR ON FIRE" on a crit | 450ms |
| MARKET | market | Ballista | Big bolt on a high arc, heavy screen shake on impact | 1000ms |
| MARKET | wtp | Coin cannon | Burst of gold coins that bounce | 700ms |
| MOAT | differentiation | Laser rifle | Instant straight beam with afterglow | 150ms |
| MOAT | moat | Trebuchet | Boulder on a very high arc. Parry raises a stone wall. | 1100ms |
| MOAT | timing | Clockwork railgun | Three tick charge-up, then a fast slug with gear particles | 300ms + 450ms charge |
| EXECUTION | gtm | Gatling gun | 6 to 10 small rounds in a stream | 600ms |
| EXECUTION | feasibility | Rocket | Wobbly path, smoke trail | 900ms |
| EXECUTION | scale | Orbital beam | Column of light from the sky onto the target | 500ms + 300ms warning |

Projectile size scales with impact: impact 1 is 0.7x, impact 2 is 1x, impact 3 is 1.4x.

### Shot outcomes

The shot cue's `outcome` field picks the treatment. Damage always comes from the cue; the effects only show it.

| Outcome | Visual | Sound |
|---|---|---|
| `hit` | Impact flash, target hit-flash, damage number in the corner colour, ghost drain | Weapon hit |
| `crit` | As `hit`, plus impact 3 particles, 80 ms hit-stop, 6 px shake, camera at 1.13x, `CRITICAL` stamp | Heavy hit |
| `partial` | Smaller burst, damage number in `--sun-2` | Light hit |
| `parried` | Hex shield (stone wall in the moat round) in front of the defender, `PARRIED` stamp, the defense move and its headline called out on the defender's side, deck reads `NO DAMAGE` | Clang |
| `glancing` | Sparks off the armour, `GLANCING`, 0 damage | Ping |
| `whiff` | The shot sails past the shoulder, `MISS` | Whoosh |
| `backfire` | Weapon jams, two smoke puffs, explosion on the shooter, red `FACT-CHECKED` stamp, headline struck through, damage number on the shooter | Fizzle, then boom |
| Judges' hammer | A pixel gavel drops onto the weaker side, with the reason in the overlay | Gavel |

### Pacing

A turn slot is 3600 ms at 1x. The headline needs about two seconds to read and the verdict line about one, so the slot holds both without crowding. A round of six turns runs about 22 seconds. With the intro, the round cards, three scorecards and the finish, a match runs about 90 seconds. The clock in the controls shows total time and the scrub bar marks every turn start.

### Match sequence

1. **Walk-in**: both fighters on their piers, name cards in the bottom corners, a `TALE OF THE TAPE` stamp at the top, narration "Same build, same rubric, same judges. Only the founders differ." 2s.
2. **Round title**: five-band wipe, "ROUND 1 / MARKET", the three criteria and their weights. 1.5s.
3. **Turns**: the script's shot cues, one turn each, with the strip advancing.
4. **Round scorecard**: a sheet at the bottom of the stage, one row per criterion, both scores, points and HP, a column legend, and the agreement percentage in the header. 4s.
5. Repeat for rounds 2 and 3.
6. **Outcome**:
   - `KO`: the final blow plays at 0.3x with letterbox bars. The screen flashes twice (never more than 3 per second). `K.O.` stamps in and the loser dissolves into pixels carried off by the wind.
   - `DECISION`: both fighters stand. The card reads `DECISION, UNANIMOUS` or `DECISION, MAJORITY`. The loser kneels.
   - `SUDDEN_DEATH`: fog rolls in, the closings appear, then the judges' vote tally with one pip per vote.
   - `PERFECT` and `PYRRHIC VICTORY` add a stamp when the resolution says so.
7. **Winner pose** with stripe confetti and `WINNER ADVANCES`, the decided-by line, then the loser's crew walks across and an `ACQUIRED` stamp lands.
8. **Climb** plays on the Tower.

### Camera and feel

- Screen shake: amplitude in internal pixels (2 for normal hits, 6 for crits and ballistas), decays over 200 ms.
- Hit-stop only on crits and the final blow.
- The camera pushes to the actor on the call-out and to the target on impact, 1.06x to 1.13x, so the eye follows the claim and then the shot.
- The non-actor dims to 45% while a turn is live. Guard stance on a parry, hurt on a hit, staggered below 50 HP, critical below 25 HP, victory on the winner cue.
- Idle crowd silhouettes along the bottom edge bob to the beat and jump on crits. Crowd size follows clout.
- Reduced motion: no shake, no hit-stop, no flashes, no slow-mo, and the overlays appear at once. All the information still lands.

## Film room (trace viewer)

The answer to "why did this idea win?" Opens from the match screen after the match completes, or from any finished match on the Tower.

1. **Verdict header**: outcome, winner, rubric gap ("won by 13.3 rubric points"), what decided it ("Decided by: scores" or "Decided by: sudden death judge, 3 to 1"), and the three-bullet fight report.
2. **Scorecard**: one row per criterion. Columns: weight, A score (AB order, BA order, final), B score (same), margin, rubric points, HP. Stability flags inline. Clicking a row expands both judge rationales for both fighters, caps applied, and the evidence they leaned on.
3. **Timeline**: rounds, volleys, shots. Each shot shows its full claim, type, criterion, evidence chips with fact-check verdicts, judge verdict and note, and damage. Defenses link to the shot they answered.
4. **Evidence locker**: every source with URL, snippet, who found it (dossier or scouting) and which shots cited it.
5. **Raw calls**: every LLM call for the match with role, model, seed, tokens, latency, cost, and the request and response as collapsible JSON.
6. **Export**: "Copy as Markdown" so founders can take their report card home.

## Bet slip

Shown on the match screen and on Tower cards while betting is open.

- Two big buttons, one per fighter, in corner colors, each with current decimal odds ("2.40x").
- Stake chips: 10, 50, 100, 500, MAX. A custom amount field.
- Estimated payout with a note that odds move until the bell.
- A countdown to the bell. At zero the slip stamps "BETS LOCKED".
- After settlement: a coin shower on a win, a small "REKT" on a loss.
- Wallet balance in the header ("S$ 1,240").

## Host console and Arena Mode

- **Console**: tournament setup (size, preset, seeding, schedule, budget), entrant table with moderation and duplicate flags, brief approvals, weigh-in progress, per-match controls (open betting, lock, air, retry, void), eval-health numbers, spend.
- **Arena Mode**: a 16:9 full-screen view for the projector. The arena takes 70% of the width, a mini Tower on the right, a bet-volume ticker and a QR code to join along the bottom. Sound on by default. Keyboard: `B` opens betting on the next match, `L` locks, `Space` airs, `R` replays the last match, `T` toggles the Tower.

## Accessibility

- `prefers-reduced-motion`: no shake, no hit-stop, no flashes, no slow-mo. Projectiles become quick fades. The fight still reads through captions and bars.
- Flashes stay at or under 3 per second (WCAG 2.3.1).
- Captions are always on in the arena.
- Corners are distinguished by color, side and icon.
- An `aria-live="polite"` region announces hits: "Fogcast takes 18 damage on market size. Fogcast at 49."
- The Tower, the film room and the bet slip are fully keyboard-navigable.
- Contrast: text on `--paper` uses `--ink` or `--ink-3`. Never put body text on the sun ramp.

## Sound

- 8-bit SFX per weapon and per outcome, a crowd loop, a heartbeat for sudden death.
- Announcer (v1): Voxtral TTS lines pre-generated per match at seal time ("Round one. Market!", "PermitPilot with the ballista!", "K.O.!"). Pre-generating keeps playback on the shared clock.
- Muted by default on the web, unmuted in Arena Mode.

## Performance

- The canvas runs at a fixed 480x270 resolution. A particle pool caps at about 400 live particles. A low-power mode drops particles to 25% when the frame time stays above 20ms.
- Pause the render loop when the tab is hidden. On return, re-sync from the broadcast clock by applying missed cues instantly.
- Lazy-load PixiJS only on the match screen and in Arena Mode.
